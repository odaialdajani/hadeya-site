How it works

Four quiet steps between choosing a gift and a knock at their door.

1

Choose a gift

Browse curated gifts in the Hadeya app and pick one — or let Hadeya choose. No account is needed to browse, choose or pay.

2

Pay securely

Payment is handled by Moyasar, a licensed Saudi payment provider, on its own hosted page. Hadeya never sees your card number.

3

Share a private link

We create a private claim link for your gift. You pass it on however you like — we never ask you for the recipient's address.

4

They answer, we deliver

The recipient opens the link and tells us — privately — where and when to deliver. We buy the gift, wrap it, and bring it to their door.

Three promises the product is built on

Privacy is not a setting here. It is the product.

The address stays private

The sender never receives the recipient's address. This is not a policy choice we could quietly reverse — the database itself refuses it.

Private answers stay private

The recipient's address, delivery timing, sizes and preferences are never shown to the sender.

The price is always hidden

The recipient is never shown what a gift cost. There is no setting, and no way to turn it off.

The app

Hadeya is an iPhone app for Saudi Arabia, in English and Arabic, preparing for public launch.

Every gift is bought fresh on demand — we hold no warehouse stock — and every delivery is split across two separate drivers, so no single person knows both where a gift came from and who it is going to.
